Project Introduction

Our group project focuses on the material and information flows of fast fashion waste in global logistics and digital media, exploring its state of ecology in the Global South. As media practitioners, we record the material transportation trajectories of the fast fashion supply chain by building a digital archive (website and digital mapping) and choosing images, data, and prose poetry as information carriers.

When we place ourselves within this global chain, we also pay attention to the digital space parallel to this transport trajectory. The social media often uses aesthetic rhetoric to filter out the real trauma in the Global South. This visual landscape not only creates an unequal division of labor through data control but also disconnects individual actions from their material consequences in remote areas. We must ask: how does a single click in the Global North media platform trigger a chain of pollution in the Global South?

Overall, through multimedia mapping, this website reveals the covered-up, friendship-like symbiotic and entangled relationships between humans, technology, and non-human ecologies behind seemingly smooth digital consumption, aiming to present the ecological traces and damaged local memories that have been obscured by consumerist culture.
