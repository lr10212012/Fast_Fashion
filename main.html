Chapter · 01

I was born in a carefully tailored illusion.

I · Origin

At first, I was a cotton plant rooted in the soil of my homeland.
A pair of hands pulled me up by the roots.
I took shape in the roar of the machines,
stitching together the anxiety of survival.

II · Stitches

Those stitches and fingertips turning in the shadows,
connected the other end of the world to this one,
sewn into the midnight of Bangladeshi child laborers,
and sewn into the newly launched imaginations.

III · Shelf

I was placed on the shelf, waiting to be sold,
put into a brand-new package,
like a carefully pampered prisoner.
My prison is made of countless transparent walls.

IV · Screen

The digital screen is a huge filter,
I am the production of consumption symbols,
the shadow of the algorithm,
I am presented as colourful fragments,
encoded into precise traffic data,
filtered out the scars of the Himalayas, and the sounds of broken labor.
Hiding the sighs of my homeland soil into a silent corner.

V · Wasteland

I once passed through a forest built of polyester,
where rivers weeping colourful chemical tears,
dyes peeling off, mottled like the texture of a mine,
covering the collapsed factories in Cambodia, covering the dried-up riverbeds,
and that wasteland in the distance, continuously rising and made of old things.

When the season's algorithm changes again,
in every fold, a sigh is hidden.
I feel like a fleeting illusion.

When you finally reunite with me on this abandoned wasteland,
at that moment, I am the only true visibility.

Note · 04

In the fashion section of social media platforms (taking Xiaohongshu as an example), aesthetic categories are widely utilised. Bloggers simplify complex clothing combinations into individual specific visual tags, such as #OOTD, #MaillardStyle, #Fashionista, and #PlusSizeOutfit, providing plug-and-play styling templates. This phenomenon makes fashion resemble digital code that can be rapidly copied and replaced. Platform officials, by transforming online aesthetic content into offline fashion week rituals, not only reinforce the symbolic value of fashion trends but also leverage this immersive experience to increase user engagement and promote the professionalism of the platform.

Regarding the update speed and lifecycle of topics on social media platforms: based on the engagement rate (ER) formula = (likes + saves + comments) / total followers (or estimated views), a comparison of different samples from top-tier KOLs reveals that vacation styling achieves an engagement rate of 2.4%, significantly higher than the 0.19% for ordinary daily-life posts. When the content recommendation algorithm shifts from daily content to providing detailed shopping guides, the user's motivation for participation changes from simple visual appreciation to functional consolidation, verifying the strong intervention of templated styling on user decision-making.

Note · 06

Taking fashion colour trend predictions on social media platforms as an example, acting as both seasonal consumption guides and content buildup for national shopping festivals, this forecasting of styling colours and overall aesthetics indicates that social media has gradually compressed the rhythm of fashion from annual cycles into seasonal or even monthly ones. By pre-empting future consumption desires, the platforms declare current items obsolete at the symbolic level before any physical wear occurs, mandating prefabricated fashion to maintain production line turnover.

Note · 07

In the fast fashion review narratives on social media platforms (Xiaohongshu), the repeated emphasis on keywords such as low unit price essentially constructs a kind of cheap temptation. By lowering the economic threshold, this marketing strategy creates an illusion for the target audience that they can follow trends painlessly and have the capital for trial and error. However, this pathological pursuit of cheapness is the very power source that drives the continuous acceleration of fast fashion production lines and the frantic consumption of resources.

Note · 08

In the niche field of outdoor clothing and environmental issues on social media platforms, a mid-tier KOL reviewed the brand's marketing campaign involving fireworks on the Himalayas: even in the face of widespread negative public opinion, the brand's performance increased instead of decreasing six months later. The analysis points out that the media filtered public anger and reshaped the brand narrative, making ecological costs a negligible operating expense. The view in the review completely objectifies consumers, assuming that ecological reflection will be entirely dissipated by capital and transformed into consumption power, thereby constituting a collusion between media, brands, and consumers.
